### Flight Software Workshop

**Johns Hopkins APL, Maryland, United States · March 2026**

Attended the [19th Annual Flight Software Workshop](https://flightsoftware.org/workshop/FSW2026) at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory, with talks on spacecraft software, AI, autonomy, mission operations, and future space systems.

I also joined a restricted visit of selected APL facilities, including mission control, spacecraft qualification test beds, and a presentation and prototype flight-test demonstration for NASA’s Dragonfly mission, an autonomous, nuclear-powered octocopter scheduled to launch toward Titan in 2028.
